# Workbench assets

- `mcqueen.webp`: cut-out of the existing project-owned `../mcqueen-car.png` photograph.
  The original remains unchanged. Polygon mask: `scripts/prepare-bench.py`.
- `sra-board.webp`: cut-out of the SRA board repository render:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SRA-VJTI/sra-board-hardware-design/master/documentation/images/board_images/front.png
  Source license: MIT, retained in `LICENSE-SRA.txt`.
- `pliers.webp`: white-background removal from Wikimedia Commons `File:Tool-pliers.jpg`.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tool-pliers.jpg
  Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/51/Tool-pliers.jpg
  Commons metadata marks it public domain, with attribution not required (checked 2026-11-13).
- `jetson.svg`, `motor.svg`, `perfboard.svg`, `screwdriver.svg`, `solder.svg`: original SVG
  illustrations for this site. They are visual props, not wiring diagrams or product photos.
- `dumbbells.svg`, `football.svg`, `volleyball.svg`, `shuttle.svg`, `knight.svg`,
  `paddles.svg`, `uno-q.svg`: original dimensional SVG illustrations for the personal scene.
  Rubber/leather pores, knurled steel, plastic lattice, carved surfaces, and gradients are
  drawn locally. No third-party sports product images, logos, or package dependencies.
  The UNO Q is a stylised illustration, not a pinout or electrical instruction.
- Paper, notebook, phone, solder spool, screws, noise, and loose wires: original HTML/CSS/SVG.
  The phone, SRA board, pliers, and excess tools are no longer rendered; their earlier assets
  and licenses are retained rather than discarded. Props do not imply every component was
  on McQueen. On-screen board effects have no hardware connection.

No assets were copied from the design references. All runtime assets are served locally.
